About SubstiFlow

Smarter substitutions.Smoother academic flow.

SubstiFlow is a centralized academic coordination platform built to simplify faculty substitution, reduce timetable conflicts and support faster administrative decisions when a teacher is unavailable.

← Back to Home & Login

Key Features

The system brings the core substitution workflow together so faculty availability, timetable constraints and administrative decisions can be handled in one place.

Faculty Timetables

View faculty schedules, occupied periods, classes and timetable flow in one place.

Smart Substitution

Check faculty availability and timetable constraints before recommending a substitute.

HOD Approval

Keep final substitution decisions under administrative review and control.

Reports & History

Track substitution activity, actions, workload and historical records.
